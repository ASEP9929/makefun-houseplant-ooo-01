A make.fun entry for [The Houseplant Out-of-Office](https://make.fun/bounty/u-eager-stork-81-9647-the-houseplant-out-of-office-7c586047): the funniest out-of-office auto-reply a houseplant would send, in 120 characters or fewer.

## The line

> Out of office. No water since March and I've never been more at peace. The cactus has my inbox. Good luck.

The plant has been ignored since March — and it turns out neglect was the promotion. The inbox goes to the cactus, who is even less available.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the reply to `/output/entry.txt`. It runs on Node 22:

```sh
node main.ts
```
